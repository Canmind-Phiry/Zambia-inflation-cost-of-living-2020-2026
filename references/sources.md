## Data notes
The monthly CPI series covers January 2020 to October 2026 (82 observations). CPI indices are transcribed from the cited ZamStats publications; derived food and non-food inflation rates and rebased indices are calculated from the component indices. Exchange-rate and JCTR observations are documented separately in the working paper and are not included as complete monthly series in the CPI CSV.
